The Main Update: What Happened?

On May 11, 2026, Eileen Wang, the mayor of Arcadia, California, resigned from her position after the US Department of Justice announced charges against her for acting as an unregistered foreign agent. According to federal prosecutors, Wang, 58, agreed to plead guilty to one felony count of acting in the United States as an illegal agent of a foreign government without prior notification to the Attorney General.

The charges state that from late 2020 to 2022, Wang worked with a colleague, Yaoning "Mike" Sun, to promote the interests of the People's Republic of China (PRC). The pair operated a news website called "U.S. News Center," which targeted the local Chinese American community. They were allegedly instructed by Chinese government officials to post content favorable to Beijing.

In one cited instance from June 2021, Wang shared an article on her site within minutes of receiving it from a PRC official via an encrypted WeChat group. The article defended China's actions in Xinjiang and denied allegations of genocide and forced labor. Federal officials stated that Wang's conduct ceased after she was sworn into office in December 2022. She faces up to 10 years in federal prison. Her attorneys stated that she takes responsibility for "past personal mistakes" and apologized to the community.

Background Context

Arcadia is a city of about 53,000 people located northeast of Los Angeles, known for its high concentration of Chinese American residents. Eileen Wang was elected to the city council in November 2022 and later became mayor as part of a rotating system among council members.

Her colleague, Yaoning "Mike" Sun, had already pleaded guilty to the same charge in October 2024 and was sentenced to four years in federal prison. Sun was also listed as the treasurer for Wang's 2022 campaign. Additionally, Wang had communicated with John Chen, another individual convicted of acting as an illegal agent for China and sentenced to 20 months in prison.

This case is part of a broader crackdown by US authorities on foreign influence and transnational repression. The Justice Department has emphasized that individuals acting on behalf of foreign governments must register under the Foreign Agents Registration Act (FARA) or notify the Attorney General, ensuring transparency in their activities.

Conclusion: What Happens Next?

Wang is expected to formally enter her guilty plea in federal court in the coming weeks. The Arcadia City Council is set to select a new mayor from among its remaining members at the next meeting.
